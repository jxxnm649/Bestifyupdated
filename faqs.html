Frequently Asked Questions ❓

How do I track my order? ▾
Go to Account → Orders to see live status for every order you've placed.
What is the return policy? ▾
Return policy varies by product and is shown on each product's page — look for the "Return Policy" badge.
How do I withdraw money from my wallet? ▾
Go to Account → Wallet → Withdraw Funds, enter your UPI ID or bank details, and submit. Our team processes requests within 1–3 business days.
How do I become a supplier on Bestify? ▾
Open the "More" menu and tap "Become a Supplier" — fill in your shop details and our team will review your application.
I need help with something else. ▾
Use the "Chat with Us" option in the More menu, or call us directly at 9110690727.
